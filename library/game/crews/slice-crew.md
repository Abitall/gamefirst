# slice-crew

C02 buys one thing: a slice a stranger can finish and a G2 decision can read. It forms on the G1 keep line, disbands when the human answers G2, and never becomes a standing council.

## Trigger

G1 keeps the loop and it already plays start to finish; the slice adds the frame the loop lacks. One crew per slice. Not for content, polish, release, or a new system: each has its own skill and budget. The question is the frame, not the loop: G1 already judged fun.

## Members

- A01 partner leads: owns the clock, STATE.md, the gate line, the packet; routes work.
- A02 designer owns the slice question and the nine-point DoD checklist; no code.
- A03 engineer builds the frame and runs verify (E03); implements, never redesigns.
- A04 critic stays out until the build and the note are closed, then reads blind (R7).

## Input

Five inputs or no crew: a dated G1 keep line; the loop playing start to finish with S09 values in the real config; a written slice budget in STATE.md, numbers and cut list (R9); a build command; three to five strangers booked.

## Work

1. A02 writes the checklist first, one check per S10 point and no tenth point; a point that needs a new system goes to BACKLOG.
2. A03 builds the frame around the unchanged loop: title and menu, a goal ten minutes out, one progression step, win and loss, sound, save, settings, one build that runs on a clean machine.
3. Content is reused at least twice; no new art pipeline, no polish pass (R9).
4. A01 runs the 3-5 stranger sessions silently, to S10's protocol, and keeps the log (R8).
5. A02 writes the D07 note from A01's log; A01 assembles the packet.
6. A04 reads the closed set: checklist against evidence, the loop as it played, scope against the budget; findings only, no edits (R7).

## Output

The G2 packet carries the artifacts (R1): checklist with evidence per point, D07 note, build, capture, questions with two options each. A failed point is reported as failed. Each critic finding is fixed or listed as open before the packet leaves; no second review (R7). The crew recommends; the human answers proceed, fix, or pivot in one line with a date. A fix sends the named points back for one round; a pivot ends the crew. A01 writes the line to DECISIONS.md and updates STATE.md; the crew disbands.

## Limits

No publish or store work (R4); new ideas go to BACKLOG. No stranger, no packet (R8). Retuning the loop goes back to S09, not into the slice. No separate crew report: the packet is the record. Unattended work runs under an R10 contract or stops.
