# R7 — Diverge before converge

For any creative choice — concept, core loop, mechanic, level, visual direction, player-facing name — widen the set before you narrow it; judgment begins only after the set is closed.

## Why

The first idea is the most available, not the best. Judge it immediately and the set never exists; every later decision then defends a choice that was never compared.

## The set

- At least **20** variations for one choice, written down, before any is judged.
- Include **3 bad or absurd** ones on purpose: they mark the edge and often carry the useful fragment.
- No critique during generation — no score, no "but", no backlog entry. The critic (A04) speaks only after the set is closed.
- Then bring distant references — film, music, architecture, nature — and only after that compare with games. A game reference imports its genre; a film reference imports a feeling.
- Close with one `spark` pass: 10 variants, cheap and unranked, to loosen what the first 20 settled.

S06 runs this; R7 is why it is not optional.

## Tinker budget

Keep **10–15%** of creative time free of questions (R2), verdicts, and backlog entries. The output is an artifact and one line: "what surprised me". An empty line is a result: the space was not explored. Ten percent of a week is half a day: put it on the calendar, not in the gaps.

## Alive but illegible

A concept can be right before it is explainable. At G1, if something is clearly there but you cannot yet say why, take the one extension; kill only when nothing is there, not because the answer is unclean in 48 hours. An extension is a dated decision, not a delay. Record the doubt beside the concept.

## Interaction budget

At G2 count links, not systems: **three systems with ten links beat ten systems without**. A link is one system reading or writing another's state. Emergence is where surprises come from (S08).

## Record

Write the surprising result in the S06 brief, not only the winner: what appeared that no variant predicted. That is the asset the next choice inherits.

## Check

S06 produces the set and the spark pass; A04 checks that judgment began after it closed; G1 and G2 apply the budgets. The evidence is the artifact: 20 written variants, a spark pass, and one surprise line per tinker block.
